# The Corridor Stack — unifying thesis (2026-09-13)
One asset, four layers of value, one database, one truck, one desk.
The asset: a BC industrial site under policy pressure (mill, mine, yard, shop, fabricator).
| Layer | What the site has | What we do | Fee | Who competes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Paper | Surtax paid on US imports since Mar 2025; stranded US export channel | Remission / drawback / refund claims; matchmaking stranded exports to CPTPP/CETA buyers | 20–30% contingency; 2–5% commission | Big consultancies (won't touch SMEs) |
| 2 Metal | Scrap, carbide, alloys generated daily | Route collection + agency into India/Japan | spread + 2% | Yards that can't identify it |
| 3 Iron | Transformers, switchgear, gensets when it closes | Broker to data-centre EPCs before the auction | 5–10% | Auction houses, surplus dealers |
| 4 Power | A transmission-level grid interconnection, water, land, fibre, cold climate | Introduce the site to a data-centre developer as a "powered mill" | 1–3% finder / option | Nobody in BC yet |
The database: every visit logs imports, exports, scrap streams, equipment, and MW of interconnection. Every new proclamation (US 338/232/301, Canadian surtax, EU WSR, China MOFCOM) is a query against it. FOUNDER reads the gazette the day it lands; PK is at the site the same week.
Why it works: the cost structure. A human consultancy can't chase a $60k refund or a 2 MW pad-mount; an AI desk with one principal can, and the SME long tail is where the unclaimed money is.
Calendar of known shocks: Sep 29 (US import bans), Nov 10 (China magnet controls resume), Nov 21 (EU list), Q4 (Northwood closes; US refined-copper decision), May 21 2027 (EU scrap ban), Jun 27 2027 (Canada TRQ expiry).
Layer-4 evidence: DCD Aug 2026 — US pulp/paper fleet holds >10 GW of interconnected capacity across ~220 sites; power availability is the #1 site criterion (CBRE 2026); mills under pressure sell or ground-lease cheap; precedents: Stora Enso Kvarnsveden 600 MW, Southland Paper TX 2.1 GW, Wisconsin Rapids mill CUP Apr 2026, Norske Skog LOI Feb 2026. BC: Northwood (Prince George) closes Q4 2026. **Unverified:** Northwood's interconnection size and BC Hydro's stance on large-load data centres.
