Questions we get on every call

My broker already filed. Why would I need you?

Good. Ask them two things: did every shipment go in, including the older ones that need a different route, and has the money actually arrived? A large share of approved refunds never get paid for reasons that have nothing to do with eligibility. We check for free; if it is all done, we say so.

We claimed CUSMA. We didn't pay.

Then your own exposure is small. Two questions remain: did you buy US equipment or materials since March 2025 and pay Canadian surtax on it, and do you have US customers who cleared your goods themselves? For many exporters the second is the larger number, and the introduction from you is what makes it work.

Is this real?

The Supreme Court ruling is Learning Resources v. Trump, February 20, 2026, and US Customs has been paying refunds since spring. Both are public record. You never pay before you are paid, and you can speak to us before you send anything.

How long does it take?

Quarters, not weeks. Government processing and ongoing litigation both add time. We report monthly and never promise a date.

Which tariffs are covered?

The tariffs imposed under the emergency-powers statute the Court struck down — including the 2025 duties on Canadian goods. Several other tariff programs are not affected by the ruling. The findings sheet tells you exactly which of your payments are in scope.

Who actually files?

Licensed customs brokers in the United States and Canada. Our desk prepares the analysis and the file, manages the deadlines, and produces your documents. Where a matter needs counsel, we bring counsel.
